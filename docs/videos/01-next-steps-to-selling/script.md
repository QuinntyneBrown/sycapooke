# 01 · Next steps to selling on Sycapooke

Hello, and welcome. This little film is just for you. It shows the next steps to open your shop for real, so customers can buy your treasures and the money arrives in your bank. You don't need to be technical. Sit back, and pause whenever you like.

Here is where we are. Your shop is built, and it is already on the internet at www dot sycapooke dot com. It looks lovely. But right now it can't take money. Three things are still missing: your treasures, a payment account, and a few finishing touches from your technical partner.

## The road ahead

There are five steps. One: get your treasures ready, with photos and a few notes. Two: open a Stripe account, so you can be paid. Three: your technical partner connects everything. Four: you put your treasures on the site. Five: you sell, you ship, and you get paid.

Steps one and two are yours, and you can start them today. Step three is your partner's job. Let's go one at a time.

## Step one: photograph your treasures

Start with ten or twenty of your favourite pieces. Your phone's camera is all you need.

Take photos in daylight, near a window, but out of strong sun. Lay the piece on a plain background, like a white sheet or a wooden table. Show the whole piece first. That first photo becomes the cover in the shop.

Then take a few close ups. The maker's mark. The clasp or the label. Any little chips or wear. Honest photos build trust, and customers love the details. You can add up to eight photos for each treasure.

## Step one, continued: a note card for each piece

Next, for each treasure, write a short note. A notebook is perfect. You'll need six things.

A name, like gold filigree brooch. Its story: where it came from and why it's special. The maker or brand, if you know it. Its condition, such as used, excellent. A price, anywhere from one dollar to five thousand dollars. And a shipping cost. You can choose free shipping, or a flat amount up to one hundred dollars.

A tip for shipping. Pack one piece in its box, weigh it at the post office, and ask what it costs to send across the country. Use that as your shipping cost for similar pieces.

## Step two: open your Stripe account

Now, getting paid. Stripe is a well known company that takes your customers' card payments safely and sends the money to your bank. Card numbers never touch our website. Stripe looks after all of that.

There's no monthly fee. As of November 2026, Stripe's standard rate for American cards is two point nine percent, plus thirty cents, for each sale. So on a one hundred dollar sale, you keep about ninety six dollars and eighty cents. Sycapooke itself takes no commission at all. Your partner can check stripe dot com slash pricing for the latest rates.

Before you start, gather these things. Your email address and mobile phone. Your home address and date of birth. Your Social Security number. And your bank's routing number and account number. You'll find both at the bottom of one of your cheques.

Then, on your computer, go to stripe dot com and press Start now. Enter your email, your name, and a password, and confirm your email. Stripe will then ask about your business. Choose individual, or sole proprietor. For the website, enter www dot sycapooke dot com. Describe what you sell: antiques and vintage jewelry, clothing, housewares and electronics. Finally, add your bank account. Stripe sends your money there.

Please turn on two step sign in when Stripe offers it. It sends a code to your phone, so nobody else can get into your account.

## Step two, continued: invite your partner

Here's the most important part. Never send passwords or secret codes by email or text message. Instead, invite your partner into your Stripe account. Open Settings, then Team, and press Add member. Type your partner's email address, choose the Developer role, and press Invite.

That's it. Your partner can then connect Stripe to the shop, without you copying anything. And you stay the owner.

## Step three: what your partner does

While you take photos, your partner has four jobs. You don't need to do these, but it helps to know about them, so you can ask how they're going.

First, connect Stripe to the shop, so the checkout can take real payments. Second, switch on real email. Today, the shop's emails don't reach anyone yet. Customers need them to confirm their accounts, and you need them to hear about sales. Third, make you the shop's owner. And fourth, do a real test: buy something small, then refund it, to be sure the money flows the right way.

A word on sales tax. Ask an accountant whether you must collect it in your state. If you do, Stripe has a tool for it that your partner can switch on, for a small extra fee.

## Step four: join and add your treasures

Now the fun part. On your computer or phone, go to app dot sycapooke dot com. Press Join. Enter your email and a password. Then open the email from Sycapooke, and press the link to confirm it.

Tell your partner you've joined. Once they make you the owner, you'll see a new button at the top of the page: Sell an item.

Press Sell an item. First, add your photos. Press Add photos and choose them from your phone or computer. The first one is the cover. Then pick the category, like jewelry, and the type, like brooches and pins. The size is always vintage. The shop fills that in for you.

Next, copy in your note card: the name, the story, the maker, the condition, the price, and the shipping cost.

When everything looks right, press Publish. Your treasure is in the shop straight away. Not finished? Press Save draft, and come back to it later. You can publish up to twenty treasures a day.

To change a price later, or take a piece down, open Inventory at the top of the page.

## Step five: sell, ship, and get paid

When someone buys a treasure, Stripe takes their payment, and the piece is marked sold, so nobody else can buy it. You'll get an email, and the order appears under Sales.

Open the order. It shows the customer's address, and the date to ship by. You have five business days. If a piece isn't shipped in time, the order is cancelled and the customer gets their money back automatically, so please keep an eye on it.

Pack the treasure with care and take it to the post office. Keep the receipt with the tracking number. Then open the order, press Add tracking, choose the carrier, such as USPS, type the tracking number, and press Mark as shipped. The customer is sent the tracking link by email.

And the money? Stripe sends it to your bank automatically, usually within a few business days. You can see every payment in Stripe, on your computer, or in the Stripe app on your phone.

If a customer has a problem, it appears under Support. You read what they say, then choose: give a full refund, or close it without one, with a kind explanation.

## Tell the world

Once your first treasures are in the shop, share it. Tell your friends and family. Post photos of your favourite pieces on Facebook or Instagram, with the address www dot sycapooke dot com. Every treasure has a story, and people love to hear them.

## Recap

So, here is your list for this week. Photograph ten treasures and write a note card for each. Open your Stripe account, and invite your partner as a Developer. Then, when your partner says it's ready, join the shop and publish your first treasure.

You've built something beautiful. Now let's share it. Thank you for watching.
