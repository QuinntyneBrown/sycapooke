# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sycapooke is a peer-to-peer marketplace made only for vintage: jewelry, clothing, housewares and electronics, every piece at least 20 years old and listed by a real person with a real story. Families downsizing a lifetime of treasures join to sell them, and the people who will love them next join to buy. It is a full-stack, responsive web application with an Angular frontend and a .NET (ASP.NET Core) backend.

Each L1 below is refined into testable L2 requirements in [L2.md](L2.md).

## L1-001: Open membership
Anyone aged 18 or over shall be able to create a Sycapooke account, verify it, manage their profile and close it. Every verified member shall be able to both buy and sell, with no seller approval step.

## L1-002: Authentication and access control
The system shall authenticate members securely, manage their sessions, let them recover access to their account, and allow each member to act only on the data and actions that belong to them.

## L1-003: Vintage-locked catalog
The system shall accept only vintage pieces, at least 20 years old, in the categories Sycapooke supports (jewelry, clothing, housewares, electronics); every listing carries the one accepted size value, Vintage (a vintage lock, [ADR general/0006](../adr/general/0006-vintage-only.md)). The backend shall enforce this rule for every listing, whatever the client sends.

## L1-004: Listing management
Sellers shall be able to create, photograph, describe, price, publish, edit and withdraw listings for new and used items, and see all of their listings in one place.

## L1-005: Catalog browsing and search
Anyone, signed in or not, shall be able to browse the catalog of active listings and narrow it by keyword, category, brand, condition and price.

## L1-006: Listing detail
Anyone shall be able to view a single listing with everything needed to decide whether to buy it, including its photos, condition, price, shipping cost and seller reputation.

## L1-007: Purchasing and payment
Verified members shall be able to buy a listed item, pay for it securely through a third-party payment provider, and be guaranteed that each item is sold exactly once. Sellers shall be paid once the sale is complete.

## L1-008: Order fulfilment
The system shall track every order from payment through shipping and delivery to completion, and shall handle cancellations, refunds and problems with an order.

## L1-009: Ratings and reviews
Each member shall have a public reputation made of a rating summary of published reviews. Writing reviews after a sale is deferred; see [ADR 0001](../adr/general/0001-defer-moderation-reviews-and-data-export.md).

## L1-010: Member profiles
Each member shall have a public profile that shows their listings, ratings and reviews without exposing private contact or address details.

## L1-011: Notifications
The system shall tell members, by email, about events that affect their account, their listings and their orders.

## L1-013: Security
The system shall protect members, their data and their money against the OWASP Top 10 and other common web application threats.

## L1-014: Privacy and data protection
The system shall collect only the personal data it needs, keep it private, protect it at rest and in transit, and let members erase their data.

## L1-015: Performance and scalability
The system shall respond quickly on phones and desktops and shall keep doing so as the number of members and listings grows.

## L1-016: Responsive design
Every page and dialog shall work on extra small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium (>=768px), large (>=992px) and extra large (>=1200px) viewports.

## L1-017: Accessibility
The web application shall be usable by people with disabilities and shall conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## L1-018: Reliability and observability
The system shall be highly available, recover from failure without losing data, report errors consistently, and give operators the logs, metrics and alerts they need to run it.

## L1-019: Developer workspace
A developer shall be able to clone the repository and, with one command, restore, build, lint and test the backend solution and the frontend workspace on Windows and on Linux. A developer shall also be able to reset a local install and load demo data with one command.

## L1-020: Marketing page
The web application shall have a public marketing page that explains Sycapooke to shoppers and sellers in short copy, a hero photograph, the founder's story and a short pitch video that plays only when the visitor chooses, shows a preview image when its link is shared, and leads each audience to the catalog or to selling. It shall say that everything on Sycapooke is vintage (jewelry, clothing, housewares and electronics, every piece at least 20 years old, listed by a real person with a real story) and shall never promise new, reproduction or "vintage-style" goods.
