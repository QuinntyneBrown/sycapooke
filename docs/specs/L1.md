# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sycapooke is a web shop for its founder, one older Caribbean woman, to sell her own antiques and vintage treasures with their stories. She is the sole seller. Customers can browse and create private accounts to buy; they cannot create public profiles or sell. The collection covers jewelry, clothing, housewares and electronics, every piece at least 20 years old. Here, "antiques and vintage treasures" retains that 20-year minimum rather than introducing a 100-year threshold. It is a full-stack, responsive web application with an Angular frontend and a .NET (ASP.NET Core) backend.

This product definition supersedes the inherited public marketplace model; see [ADR general/0010](../adr/general/0010-founder-only-shop.md). These requirements define the target behavior; the application, mocks, designs and estimates require separate alignment work.

Each L1 below is refined into testable L2 requirements in [L2.md](L2.md).

## L1-001: Private customer accounts and founder access
Anyone aged 18 or over shall be able to create a private customer account, verify it, manage their account details and close it. Registration shall grant buying access only. Only the founder, whose management access is provisioned by a trusted operator, shall sell or manage the shop; no public flow shall grant that access.

## L1-002: Authentication and access control
The system shall authenticate customers and the founder securely, manage their sessions and let them recover account access. Customers shall access only their own private account and purchases. Shop management, fulfilment and customer-support actions shall be restricted to the founder.

## L1-003: Vintage-locked catalog
The system shall accept only vintage pieces, at least 20 years old, in the categories Sycapooke supports (jewelry, clothing, housewares, electronics); every listing carries the one accepted size value, Vintage (a vintage lock, [ADR general/0006](../adr/general/0006-vintage-only.md)). The backend shall enforce this rule for every listing, whatever the client sends.

## L1-004: Founder inventory management
Only the founder shall be able to create, photograph, describe, price, publish, edit and withdraw listings for her eligible antiques and vintage treasures, and see all of her inventory in one place.

## L1-005: Catalog browsing and search
Anyone, signed in or not, shall be able to browse the catalog of active listings and narrow it by keyword, category, brand, condition and price.

## L1-006: Listing detail
Anyone shall be able to view a single treasure with everything needed to decide whether to buy it, including its photos, story, condition, price, shipping cost and a link to the founder's public story and collection.

## L1-007: Purchasing and payment
Verified customers shall be able to buy a listed item, pay securely through the shop's third-party payment provider, and be guaranteed that each item is sold exactly once. Payments shall go directly to the founder's shop account under the provider's settlement process, without a Sycapooke marketplace commission, seller onboarding or completion-based payout release.

## L1-008: Order fulfilment
The system shall track every order from payment through shipping and delivery to completion. The founder shall ship orders, handle cancellation requests and resolve customer problems, including authorizing full refunds, through her protected management area. The shop shall not present her decisions as independent marketplace arbitration.

## L1-009: Founder rating summary
The founder's public rating summary and reviews section shall show "No reviews yet". Customers shall have no public rating or reviews section. Review authoring remains deferred under the existing scope decision.

## L1-010: Founder story and collection
The founder alone shall have a public story and collection page showing her portrait, story, eligible listings, completed-sales count and the "No reviews yet" summary. Customer accounts and purchase activity shall remain private. Public pages shall not expose private contact or address details.

## L1-011: Notifications
The system shall email customers about their accounts and purchases, and the founder about her account, inventory, sales and customer-support events.

## L1-013: Security
The system shall protect members, their data and their money against the OWASP Top 10 and other common web application threats.

## L1-014: Privacy and data protection
The system shall collect only the personal data it needs, keep private account data private, protect it at rest and in transit, and let customers erase their personal data through account closure under the retention rules. Only the founder's approved story and collection details shall be public.

## L1-015: Performance and scalability
The system shall respond quickly on phones and desktops as the number of customers and treasures grows, using efficient image delivery and stateless application services within the existing testing scope.

## L1-016: Responsive design
Every page and dialog shall work on extra small (<576px), small (>=576px), medium (>=768px), large (>=992px) and extra large (>=1200px) viewports.

## L1-017: Accessibility
The web application shall be usable by people with disabilities and shall conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## L1-018: Reliability and observability
The system shall be highly available, recover from failure without losing data, report errors consistently, and give operators the logs, metrics and alerts they need to run it.

## L1-019: Developer workspace
A developer shall be able to clone the repository and, with one command, restore, build, lint and test the backend solution and the frontend workspace on Windows and on Linux. A developer shall also be able to reset a local install and load demo data with one command.

## L1-020: Marketing page
The web application shall have a public marketing page introducing the founder, an older Caribbean woman, and her own antiques and vintage treasures through short copy, a hero photograph, her story and a short pitch video that plays only when the visitor chooses. It shall show a preview image when shared and invite visitors to shop her collection or learn her story. It shall name the four categories and the 20-year minimum and shall never invite the public to sell or promise modern, reproduction or "vintage-style" goods.
