Foundation · Heirloom

Content

Warm, respectful and brief. Talk like the grandmother who founded Sycapooke: every piece is a treasure with a story, and so is the person selling it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableHeirloom design system
  • Overview
  • Voice and tone
  • Mechanics
  • Formulas
  • Microcopy library

Overview

Sycapooke writes for families downsizing a lifetime of treasures, and for the people who’ll love them next: lead with the answer, use real makers, decades and prices, and keep it warm without being precious. Say “treasures” and “story”; never say “fast fashion”, “pre-owned bargains” or anything that sounds like a clearance. The warmth lives in the colours, the Fraunces headlines and one kind word at a milestone, not in every sentence.

Voice and tone

  • Friendly: “What are you passing on?”
  • Specific: “Vintage. Every piece on Sycapooke is at least 20 years old.”
  • Respectful: a brooch is “signed Trifari, c.1950s”, never “old costume jewelry”; a seller is a person with a story, never a stock number.
  • Reassuring in trouble: “Your draft is safe. Try again in a minute.”
  • Warm at milestones: “Your listing is live. Someone is going to love this.”

Mechanics

  • Sentence case for everything except names and brands.
  • Buttons are verbs: “Sell”, “Buy now”, “Publish listing”. Never OK/Submit/Yes.
  • Dates: “Fri 30 Oct”, “in 2 days”; prices show cents: “$18.00”.
  • The lock reads “Vintage” everywhere: the tag, the fixed size line and the aria-label.
  • Decades read “c.1950s”; makers are written as they signed: Trifari, Pyrex, Kodak, Pendleton.
  • Categories are Jewelry, Clothing, Housewares and Electronics; never copy written for children, never “fast fashion”.
  • Use “·” to join facts in meta lines.

Formulas

  • Error: what happened + what’s safe + what to do. “Couldn’t load the catalog. Try again.”
  • Field error: the fix. “Enter a price like 18.00.”
  • Empty: what this is + why empty + one action. “Nothing for sale yet. List a treasure to see it here.”
  • Success: what happened + what happens next.
