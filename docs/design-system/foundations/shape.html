Foundation · Heirloom

Shape

Three radii and the pill: 0.375rem for inputs and focus rings, 1rem for cards, wells and dialogs, 2rem for large photos, and full pills for buttons, chips, tags and avatars.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableHeirloom design system
  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Borders
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

The landing page uses exactly four corner treatments: rounded-md (0.375rem) on inputs and focus rings, rounded-2xl (1rem) on cards, photo wells, the dashed empty state and the forest card, rounded-[2rem] (2rem) on the large hero and story photos, and rounded-full on buttons, chips, pills and the search well. Heirloom maps them to --radius-sm and --radius-md (both 0.375rem), --radius-lg, --radius-xl and --radius-full; --radius-xs (0.25rem) is kept for checkboxes.

Borders

  • 1px line (--color-border-default) for edges, dividers, inputs, chips, cards and the dashed empty state.
  • 2px for the secondary button, the avatar ring, the step number ring, the quote rule and the focus ring.

Usage

  • Buttons, chips, badges, status pills, the size and category tags, the search well, switches and avatars are pills.
  • Inputs, selects, textareas and menus use --radius-md; thumbnails too.
  • Listing photo wells, cards, panels, wells, dialogs, sheets, alerts, toasts and the empty state use --radius-lg; the gallery’s main photo and the marketing photos use --radius-xl.
  • Nested shapes use a smaller radius than their container.
  • Never cut corners with clip-path or a chamfer; every shape comes from a radius token.
