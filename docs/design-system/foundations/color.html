Foundation · Heirloom

Color

The Heirloom palette — Paper, Cream, Ink, Muted, Line, Terracotta, Forest and Mustard — extracted from the Sycapooke landing page, with an ink-and-cream dark theme. Every text pairing passes WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

Overview

Heirloom’s colour structure (primitives, semantic roles, status, pops, both themes) carries eight named colours from the Sycapooke landing page: Paper (the page), Cream (surfaces and hover fills), Ink (text and dark surfaces), Muted (secondary text), Line (borders), Terracotta (primary: the tag logo, buttons, links and the Vintage tag), Forest (secondary: the forest card, eyebrows and stat labels, dark surfaces) and Mustard (highlights and accent rules). Colour has three jobs: brand (the terracotta button, price and focus ring), meaning (info, success, warning, danger) and bands (the flat paper, cream, terracotta, forest and ink surfaces of the landing page). Every band is one solid colour with its own on-colour; status colours carry meaning and always come with an icon and text.

  • Light: paper-100 canvas, cream (paper-50) surfaces, terracotta-600 accent with cream text (5.9:1), terracotta-700 links (7.3:1 on paper), ink-800 text, ink-500 muted text, paper-300 borders.
  • Dark: ink-950 canvas, ink-900 surfaces, ink-850 raised, cream (paper-50) text, paper-300 muted text, terracotta-400 accent with ink-950 text on it, mustard-300 links.
  • Bands: paper (the hero and most sections), cream under a line rule (Our story), terracotta with cream text (From my collection and the closing call to action), forest with cream text (the forest card) and ink with cream text (the footer). The terracotta, forest and ink bands keep their colours in both themes.
  • Never write a colour in a component; use a semantic token.

Named colours

Eight named colours make up the Heirloom palette. Each is one step of a full scale (below), and the semantic tokens point at them; components never use them directly. Values are read live from tokens.css.

Paper · --palette-paper-100The page canvas (--color-bg-canvas) and the hole of the tag logo.
Cream · --palette-paper-50Surfaces, hover fills and inputs (--color-bg-surface); the text on terracotta, forest and ink (--color-fg-on-accent, --color-fg-on-terracotta, --color-fg-on-forest, --color-fg-on-ink); the dark-theme text.
Ink · --palette-ink-800Text (--color-fg-default), the inverse toast and, darker, the dark-theme canvas and surfaces.
Muted · --palette-ink-500Secondary text (--color-fg-muted): meta lines, captions, ledes.
Line · --palette-paper-300Borders, dividers and hairlines (--color-border-default); the dark-theme muted text.
Terracotta · --palette-terracotta-600Primary: the tag logo, primary buttons, the Vintage tag, the current page and the focus ring (--color-accent). Terracotta text and links use terracotta-700 so they reach 7.3:1 on paper.
Forest · --palette-forest-600Secondary: the forest card and the inverse button’s hover (--color-band-forest), eyebrows and stat labels, and dark surfaces. Too dark for anything but cream text on it.
Mustard · --color-rule-mustardHighlights and accent rules: a fill only. Mustard text uses mustard-700 (4.9:1 on paper); dark-theme links use mustard-300.

Bands

The landing page stacks flat bands. Each has one background token and its on-colours; none has a gradient, a shape or a shadow. The terracotta, forest and ink bands do not change with the theme.

Paper --color-bg-canvas · ink text, muted ledes, forest eyebrows
Cream --color-bg-surface under a line rule · Our story, cards, wells, dialogs
Terracotta --color-band-terracotta · --color-fg-on-terracotta, -muted (85%) · From my collection, the closing call to action; ink buttons
Forest --color-band-forest · --color-fg-on-forest, -muted · the forest card, inverse button hover
Ink --color-band-ink · --color-fg-on-ink, -muted (70%), --color-border-on-ink (15%) · the footer, inverse buttons

The older pop tokens (--color-pop-pink-* → terracotta, --color-pop-grape-* → forest, --color-pop-sun-* → mustard; mint, sky and orange) and the hero gradient tokens stay defined for compatibility but nothing in Sycapooke draws with them, --color-fg-forest is forest text and --color-rule-mustard draws the quote rule.

Sycapooke layout tokens

Sycapooke keeps the 12L marketplace layout and components (by way of its clone origin, ADR general/0009), so a few component styles still name 12L layout tokens. Each one is an alias of a Heirloom token at the end of tokens.css; Heirloom stays the only source of the look. New code uses the Heirloom names.

  • --palette-ink-* and --palette-paper-* are Heirloom’s own ink and paper scales; --palette-stone-* → ink 200 to 700, --palette-signal-* → terracotta 300 and 600, --palette-red-800 → red-700 (resolved values below).
  • --color-brand → --color-accent (kept as an alias; components use --color-accent).
  • --photo-bg-1 … -6 → warm tints, kept as aliases; photo wells are cream (--color-bg-surface).
  • --chamfer is 0px: the 12L 45° cut is switched off because every Heirloom corner is a radius.
  • --color-band-terracotta, --color-band-forest, --color-band-ink and their --color-fg-on-* and --color-border-on-ink on-colours: the flat bands of the landing page.
  • --color-inverse-*: status icons and edges on the inverse toast.

Usage

  • One accent per view: the primary button and the current nav item. The Vintage tag and the tag logo are the only other terracotta.
  • Pops are decoration; pair them with text. Photo wells never carry meaning.
  • New brand theme: add [data-theme="name"] overriding semantic tokens only, then extend contrast-pairs.json and rerun the checker.
