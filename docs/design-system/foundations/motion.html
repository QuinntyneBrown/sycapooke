Foundation · Heirloom

Motion

Almost none. Colours change over 150 ms, listing photos zoom over 300 ms, and only spinners and skeletons loop. Nothing decorative moves.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableHeirloom design system
  • Overview
  • Durations and easings
  • Reduced motion
  • Usage
  • Tokens

Overview

The landing page animates two things: colour on hover (transition-colors duration-150 ease-out) and the listing photo’s 3% zoom on card hover (duration-300). Heirloom keeps exactly that: --duration-fast with --ease-standard for every colour change, --duration-slow for the photo zoom and for overlays entering (dialogs scale in, sheets and toasts slide up). Spinners, skeleton shimmer and the indeterminate bar loop because they report status. Nothing lifts, presses, bounces or drifts.

Durations and easings

--ease-spring remains in the token file but nothing uses it.

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion the tokens set durations to 0.01 ms and components drop animations; the photo zoom, skeleton shimmer and indeterminate bars become static. The spinner keeps one slow turn because it reports status. The Angular root also disables animations through @.disabled (L2-065 AC6).

Usage

  • Hover changes colour only: background, border or text, over --duration-fast.
  • The only transform is the listing photo’s scale(1.03) on card hover, over --duration-slow.
  • Never animate on page load except skeleton shimmer; nothing over 500 ms except loops and progress.
  • Enter with --ease-enter, exit with --ease-exit; toasts slide up, dialogs scale in from 576px and slide up below it.
