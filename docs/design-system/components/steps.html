Steps

A numbered list: each number sits in a 3rem ring of the current text colour beside a serif title and one large muted line.

Overview

sy-steps shows how buying a treasure from Sycapooke works, inside the How it works band (“Three steps. That’s it.”) of /welcome: Find a treasure, Pay securely, Receive its story.

Anatomy

  1. Number from a CSS counter in a 3rem circle with a 2 px ring in the current colour (ink on paper, cream on terracotta), Fraunces 600 at --font-size-xl
  2. Title h3 in Fraunces 600 at --font-size-xl
  3. One muted line at --font-size-lg

Variants

One variant; it takes its colours from the band it sits in.

  1. Find a treasure

    Browse jewelry, clothing, housewares and electronics.

  2. Pay securely

    Check out with a card; the piece is yours once you pay.

  3. Receive its story

    It arrives packed with care, with a note about where it came from.

On the terracotta band: cream rings and text.

Sizes

One size; it fills its container. Items are --space-10 apart.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Always one column; the ring stays 3rem and the text wraps beside it.

Theming

The ring and number use currentColor; the text follows --color-fg-muted, which the terracotta band re-points to cream/85.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An ordered list, so screen readers announce the count and order; titles are h3 under the band’s h2. The drawn number is a pseudo-element; the list order carries the meaning.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Not focusable.

Labelling

The band’s h2 names the list.

Content

A short phrase per title, one line of text.
