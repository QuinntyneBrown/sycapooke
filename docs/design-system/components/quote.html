Quote

Words someone wrote, set apart by a 2px mustard rule on the left: serif text in curly quotes and a large muted attribution. The founder’s story on the marketing page, and a member’s note on an order.

Overview

sy-quote sets text a person wrote apart from Sycapooke’s own copy, in the landing page’s style: a 2 px mustard rule, Fraunces text at --font-size-2xl (3xl from 768px) in curly quotes, and the attribution at --font-size-xl, muted (L2-035). The small size keeps the same shape for a member’s note inside a dialog.

Anatomy

  1. Left rule: 2 px, mustard (--color-rule-mustard), --space-6 of padding
  2. Text in Fraunces 400, wrapped in “ and ”
  3. Citation: cite in Source Sans at --font-size-xl, muted

Variants

“It’s the made-up word I whispered to my son when he was a baby. When it came time to clear out our family home, I realized every teacup and brooch deserved that same word — and someone who’d see it too.”

Founder, age 70, grandmother of five
Default: the founder’s story on /welcome.

“Found a crack in the heel counter while packing. Sorry about this.”

vintage_lou
Small (.quote--sm): a member’s note on a page or in a dialog body.

Sizes

Default (2xl, 3xl from 768px) and small (lg text, sm citation).

States

Static. Leave it out when the member wrote nothing; never show empty quotes.

Responsive behaviour

The text wraps at 36ch; long words break anywhere.

Theming

The mustard rule is the same in both themes; the citation follows --color-fg-muted.

Accessibility

A native blockquote with cite; screen readers announce it as a quotation. The rule is decorative.

Content

Show the text exactly as written, trimmed. The citation is the display name, or the founder’s line on the marketing page.

Do and don’t

  • Do use it only for words a person wrote.
  • Don’t quote Sycapooke’s own messages; use an alert.
