Component · Marketing

Feature band

A full-width page band on paper, terracotta or cream: forest eyebrow, serif headline, an optional action and either points or a projected body such as steps or the founder’s story.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

sy-feature-band carries every band of the marketing page after the hero: For shoppers (paper), For sellers (terracotta), How it works (paper, stacked), Our story (cream under a line rule, stacked, with sy-quote and the founder photo) and the closing call to action (terracotta, stacked). Bands are flat: one colour each, no gradient, no decoration.

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow: --text-label in forest (cream/80 on terracotta)
  2. Headline h2 in Fraunces 600 at --font-size-3xl, 3 to 6 words; an em is italic terracotta (italic cream on terracotta)
  3. Optional lede at --font-size-xl and optional actions: primary pills on paper and cream, inverse (ink, forest on hover) pills on terracotta
  4. Points (serif title and one large muted line each, over a line rule) or a projected body

Variants

default on paper, sell on terracotta with cream text, story on cream under a line rule; stacked puts the body below the heading.

Every piece is at least 20 years old.

Browse the catalog

  • Listed by a real personEvery piece comes from a family with a story to tell about it.
  • Honest conditionFive grades, and flaws named up front.
Default, on paper.

For sellers

List your first item
  • Big buttons, clear stepsPhotos, a price and the condition. That’s it.
  • A real person to callStuck? Ring 1-800-555-0123 and a person picks up.
Sell: terracotta, cream text, an ink button. “List your first item” goes to /join for a visitor or /sell for a member.

How it works

Three steps. That’s it.

  1. List it

    Add photos, a price and the condition.

  2. Sell it

    A buyer pays. You ship within 5 business days.

  3. Get paid

    The order completes and your payout is on its way.

Stacked, with sy-steps as the body.

Shop the catalog
Closing: the terracotta band with a lede and one inverse button.

Sizes

One size; it fills its container. Padding --space-16, --space-24 from 992px.

States

Static.

Responsive behaviour

Heading and body stack below 992px; 5:7 columns from 992px unless stacked.

Theming

Default and story bands follow the page tokens in both themes. The terracotta band keeps --color-band-terracotta and --color-fg-on-terracotta in both themes and re-points the muted, link, rule and focus-ring tokens to cream inside itself.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled by its h2; points are a list.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own.

Focus

Actions use the button focus ring; cream on terracotta.

Labelling

headingId labels the section.

Contrast

Point text on paper
Point text on cream
Cream on terracotta
Muted cream on terracotta
Ink button label

Motion

None.

Touch

Interactive parts meet the 44px target.

Content

Three points at most, each a few words plus one short line. Say what is vintage and who is selling it; never promise new goods, never say “fast fashion”.
