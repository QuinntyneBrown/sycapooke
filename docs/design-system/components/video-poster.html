Video poster

The hero photograph as one large button with a “Play · 0:29” pill that opens the video dialog. Nothing plays until it is pressed.

Overview

sy-video-poster is the hero photograph of /welcome as one large button: the same 4:5 frame with 2rem corners, no border and no shadow, with a cream pill bottom-left reading “Play · 0:29”. Pressing it opens the pitch video dialog through DialogService. Nothing plays or sounds until it is pressed.

Anatomy

  1. Native button filling the hero’s media column, clipped to --radius-xl (2rem) with a cream well behind the image
  2. Poster image, 4:5 and object-fit: cover, decorative (alt="") because the button is named
  3. Play pill: cream at 95% over the photo, ink text, --space-3 by --space-5, pill corners, Source Sans 3 600 at --font-size-md, the play glyph before “Play”
  4. Length “· 0:29” inside the pill in muted ink, hidden from assistive technology

Variants

One variant, in the hero’s media slot.

Default: the hero photo with the Play pill.

Sizes

One size; it fills the hero’s media column at 4:5, like the photograph it replaces.

States

Default, hover (the photo zooms to 1.03 over --duration-slow, as a listing card’s photo does) and focus (the 2 px terracotta ring, 4 px outside the frame). There is no pressed look: the dialog opens. The video never autoplays.

Hover (photo zoomed) and focus (terracotta ring).

Responsive behaviour

Below 62rem the hero stacks and the poster sits under the calls to action at full width; from 62rem it takes the right column of the 1.1fr 1fr grid. The pill stays --space-5 from the bottom-left corner at every width.

Theming

The pill is --color-bg-surface at 95% with --color-fg-default text and --color-fg-muted for the length, so in dark mode it is an ink pill with cream text over the same photograph. The well behind the image is --color-bg-surface.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native button whose accessible name is its visible label, “Play”; the length is aria-hidden and the image is decorative. It is the only control in the hero besides the two calls to action.

Keyboard

Enter and Space open the dialog; Tab order is eyebrow, calls to action, poster.

Focus

The shared 2 px terracotta ring with a 4 px offset, outside the rounded frame. Focus returns to this button when the dialog closes.

Labelling

Named “Play” by its label. Keep the hero’s h1 as the heading; the button needs none of its own.

Contrast

“Play” on the cream pill
Length on the cream pill

Motion

Only the 300 ms photo zoom on hover, which prefers-reduced-motion: reduce turns off with every other transition. Nothing plays until pressed (WCAG 1.4.2, 2.2.2).

Touch

The whole photograph is the target; the pill alone is 44 px tall.

Content

The label is the one word “Play”; the length follows as “· 0:29” in minutes and seconds. Say the length as it is: the build script sets it from the rendered video.
