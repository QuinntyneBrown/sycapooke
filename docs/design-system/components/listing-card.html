Listing card

One listing in the catalog grid: a cream photo well with the category pill, a serif title, era and place, and the terracotta price beside the seller. No border, no shadow.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.3

Overview

sy-listing-grid lays out sy-listing-cards one per row below 576 px, 2 columns from 576 px and 4 from 992 px (3 beside the filters sidebar until 1200 px; L2-061), with --space-6 between columns and --space-10 between rows. The card is the landing page’s: a square cream photo well, the title as its one link, and nothing drawn around it.

Anatomy

  1. Photo well.photo: square, cream, --radius-lg; a <picture> with explicit width and height, object-fit: cover
  2. Category pillTop-left, the category pill style (cream/95, ink, semibold); a Reserved or Sold stamp takes its place
  3. Titleh3 in Fraunces 600 at --font-size-lg; its link covers the whole card
  4. MetaEra · place in small muted text
  5. FooterPrice in Fraunces 600 terracotta beside the seller’s name in small muted text

Sizes

The card fills its grid column. The photo sizes attribute is (min-width: 1200px) 270px, (min-width: 992px) 23vw, (min-width: 576px) 48vw, 100vw, so a 375 px phone loads the 400 w version (L2-059).

Default, hover (the photo scales 3% over 300 ms; nothing else moves), focus (the ring sits 4 px outside the card) and loading. The grid shows 8 skeleton cards with aria-busy="true" and the label "Loading listings".

Responsive behaviour

Columns change at 36rem, 62rem and 75rem. The first 4 cards load eagerly with high fetch priority; the rest are lazy.

Theming

The well is --color-bg-surface (cream; ink-900 in dark), the price --color-fg-accent (terracotta-700; terracotta-300 in dark), the pill and the Reserved stamp cream/95 with ink text, Sold is --color-bg-inverse.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The grid is a ul role="list"; each card is an article with an h3. The title link is the card’s only link and is stretched over the card with a pseudo-element; the save button sits above it.

Keyboard

Tab reaches the title link, then the save button. Enter opens the listing; Space or Enter on the save button toggles aria-pressed.

Focus

The focus ring is drawn around the whole card (:has(a:focus-visible)), 4 px outside it.

Labelling

The link’s name is the title. The photo’s alt is "Photo 1 of {title}, {era}"; the save button is named "Save {title}". All text is bound as text, never HTML.

Motion

Only the photo zooms on hover (300 ms); it stops under reduced motion.

Touch

The save button is 44 px; the card is a large target.

Content

Prices are whole dollars without cents ("$85"), cents only when non-zero ("$18.50"), with thousands separators. Meta is the decade and the seller’s city.
