Component · Marketing

Marketing hero

The opening band of the marketing page, on paper: forest eyebrow, a large serif headline with an italic terracotta emphasis, one supporting line, two pill buttons and the hero photo with 2rem corners. No gradient, no shapes, no frame.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

sy-marketing-hero opens the marketing page at /welcome on the paper canvas, exactly as the landing page does. It says what Sycapooke is in a few words: a home for the things that mattered, the marketplace made only for vintage. The closing call to action is not a hero: it is the terracotta feature band.

Anatomy

  1. Eyebrow: --text-label in forest
  2. Headline h1 in Fraunces 600: --font-size-5xl, 6xl from 768px, 7xl from 992px, line height 1.05, tracking tight; the em is italic terracotta
  3. Lede: --font-size-xl, relaxed, muted
  4. Actions: a primary pill and a secondary pill, large
  5. Media: the hero photo (/media/sycapooke-hero.jpg), 4:5, --radius-xl corners, no border or shadow, in the second column from 992px

Variants

One variant.

Jewelry · Clothing · Housewares · Electronics

A home for the things that mattered.

Antiques and vintage treasures from one Caribbean woman’s own collection. Every piece is at least 20 years old.

Shop the collectionMeet the founder
“A home for the things that mattered.” Primary “Shop the collection” goes to /, secondary “Meet the founder” jumps to the Our story band on the same page.

Sizes

One size; it fills its container. Padding --space-16, --space-24 from 992px.

States

Static. The buttons keep their own hover, active and focus states.

Responsive behaviour

One column below 992px with the photo after the actions; 1.1fr + 1fr columns from 992px. The headline steps through 5xl, 6xl and 7xl.

Theming

Semantic page tokens: paper, ink, muted, terracotta and forest in light; the ink canvas with cream text in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A section labelled by its heading, the page h1.

Keyboard

No keyboard interaction of its own; the actions are native links.

Focus

The shared terracotta ring.

Labelling

headingId labels the section; the photo carries descriptive alt text.

Contrast

Headline on paper
Terracotta emphasis on paper
Lede on paper
Forest eyebrow on paper

Motion

None.

Touch

Both buttons are 48 px tall.

Content

  • Headline up to seven words with one emphasised word, one supporting sentence, two actions at most.
  • Say that everything is vintage, at least 20 years old; never promise new or modern goods, and never say “fast fashion” (ADR general/0005).
