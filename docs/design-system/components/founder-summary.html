Founder summary

The founder who sells every item: avatar, name linking to her story, completed-sales count, “No reviews yet” and a link to the story.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v0.4

Overview

sy-founder-summary closes the listing info column. Sycapooke has one seller, the founder, so there is no “New seller” variant: with no completed sales the count reads “0 completed sales”.

Anatomy

  1. Avatar with initials
  2. The founder's name, a link to her story (the profile page)
  3. "Founder of Sycapooke · {n} completed sales"
  4. "No reviews yet" (sy-rating-summary)
  5. A text link: "Read her story and see her collection"

Variants

IC
Ida CampbellFounder of Sycapooke · 12 completed salesNo reviews yetRead her story and see her collection
IC
Ida CampbellFounder of Sycapooke · 0 completed salesNo reviews yetRead her story and see her collection
Founder with 12 completed sales; with none (listing-detail/zero-sales).

Sizes

One size.

States

Any number of completed sales, including zero. The reviews line reads “No reviews yet” until reviews ship.

Responsive behaviour

The same at every width.

Theming

Avatar and badge use accent and neutral tokens.

Accessibility

The avatar is hidden from assistive technology. "No reviews yet" is plain text; the story link is a normal text link.

Content

The count uses thousands separators and is written out (“0 completed sales”, “12 completed sales”).

Do and don't

  • Do show “0 completed sales” and “No reviews yet” rather than hiding the line.
  • Don't invent a “New seller” badge, and don't show the founder's email or address.

Tokens

--color-accent-subtle, --color-fg-accent, --color-border-default, --space-4.

Code

<sy-founder-summary [founder]="shop.founder" [completedSales]="shop.completedSales" />
