Footer

The ink footer: the light logo and a line about Sycapooke, three link columns with serif headings, and a bottom bar over a faint cream rule.

Overview

The footer closes the app shell on ink with cream text, as on the landing page. Its columns carry the four categories, Start selling and Marketplace rules, and About Sycapooke (the marketing page at /welcome), Privacy, Terms and the Cookie settings entry (privacy subsystem), a link-styled button that emits cookieSettings because it opens a dialog rather than a page. The admin shell has no footer.

Anatomy

  1. Brand columnThe light sy-logo (cream wordmark) linking home and one line in cream/70
  2. Link columnsThree navs named by their serif headings (Shop, Sell, Sycapooke); links in cream/80 at --text-label, --space-3 apart
  3. Bottom bar© {year} Sycapooke and the tagline over a 1 px cream/15 rule, --space-6 tall

Variants

Not applicable: one footer.

Sizes

Not applicable: one size; --space-12 above the columns, --space-6 around the bottom bar, --layout-margin at the sides.

Responsive behaviour

One column below 992 px (brand, then the three navs, --space-12 apart); from 992 px a 1.4fr + 3 × 1fr grid. The bottom bar wraps.

Theming

Ink in both themes: --color-band-ink, --color-fg-on-ink, --color-fg-on-ink-muted and --color-border-on-ink do not change with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<footer> (contentinfo) with three navs, each labelled by its heading.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabLogo, then each link column in order and the Cookie settings button; Enter or Space on the button opens the dialog.

Focus

The shared ring in cream.

Labelling

Link text is the name; the logo link is named Sycapooke home; headings are h2 under the page h1.

Contrast

Cream text on ink
Muted cream on ink

Motion

Colour only, 150 ms.

Touch

Links are 24 px line boxes with 12 px gaps.

Content

Shop: Jewelry · Clothing · Housewares · Electronics. Sell: Start selling · Marketplace rules. Sycapooke: About Sycapooke · Privacy · Terms · Cookie settings. Bottom: © {year} Sycapooke · Treasures with a story.
